Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

SwapHair AI ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, the third parties we share it with, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Photos you provide: When you upload or capture a selfie and tap Generate (or create a Hair Brief), a downscaled copy of the image and the selected style description are transmitted over HTTPS to our secure relay server hosted on Vercel (hairswap-proxy.vercel.app), which forwards them to our AI providers listed in Section 3 to generate your result. Before the first generation, the app shows an in-app consent dialog naming the provider so you can review and agree (or decline) before any photo is sent. Our relay server does not store, cache or log your photos, and your photos are never used to train AI models.

On-device data: The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us:

  • Saved hairstyle previews (stored via SwiftData on your device)
  • App preferences (gender selection, haptic settings, favorites)
  • Usage statistics (generation count, streaks, badges)
  • Face shape analysis results (processed entirely on-device using Apple Vision framework)

Subscription data: Subscription purchases are managed by Apple and RevenueCat. We receive anonymized subscription status information (whether you have an active subscription) but do not have access to your payment details, Apple ID, or personal billing information.

Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized usage data (screen views, feature usage, crash reports) to improve the app. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

Session analytics (Microsoft Clarity): We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how people use the app, for example which buttons are tapped and where screens are confusing. Clarity records how you interact with the app (taps, scrolls and screens visited) together with basic device information such as device model, operating system and approximate country. Your photos and generated images are masked and are never recorded. This data is not linked to your name or contact details and is not used for advertising or tracking. We do not record sessions for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. Elsewhere you can switch it off at any time with the "Share anonymous usage" toggle in Settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Photo processing: Your selfie is sent to OpenAI exclusively to generate your hairstyle, beard and AI Stylist previews and, for the Hair Brief, to analyze your face shape, hair and coloring and recommend styles. It is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS, is not used to train AI models, and is deleted by OpenAI within 30 days (see Section 4).
  • Face analysis & landmarks (on-device only): Apple's Vision framework (VNDetectFaceLandmarksRequest and VNGeneratePersonSegmentationRequest) detects the position of your face landmarks — eyes, nose, mouth, and chin — and a person silhouette mask, directly on your iPhone or iPad. These are used only to check that your photo shows a clear face and to match the brightness of the AI-generated result to your original photo. The face landmarks and segmentation mask are computed in memory and never written to disk, transmitted off-device, or shared with any third party. We do not perform face recognition, biometric identification, or identity matching, and we do not create or store a face print or face template. The selfie image itself is sent to our AI providers for generation as described in Section 3.
  • Color Lab and Hair Brief hair color: The color itself is applied on your device using Core Image. To find your hair precisely, a downscaled copy of your photo is sent through our relay server to a hosted hair-segmentation model on Hugging Face, which returns only a hair mask.
  • Subscriptions: To verify your subscription status and provide premium features.
  • Analytics: To understand how features are used and improve the app experience.

3. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following processors only for the narrow purposes described. Each processor provides data protection that is the same as, or substantially equivalent to, the protection we provide.

  • OpenAI: Receives your selfie and style description over HTTPS to generate hairstyle, beard and AI Stylist previews and to produce your Hair Brief analysis. OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models and deletes it within 30 days. See openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.
  • Black Forest Labs (BFL) — FLUX: Our backup image-generation provider. If we switch generation back to BFL, it receives your selfie and style description over HTTPS solely to generate your preview, does not store photos after processing, and does not use them to train AI models. See bfl.ai/privacy.
  • Hugging Face: Hosts the hair-segmentation model used by Color Lab and the Hair Brief. Receives a downscaled copy of your photo and returns a hair mask. See huggingface.co/privacy.
  • Vercel (relay hosting): Hosts our secure API relay (hairswap-proxy.vercel.app), which forwards requests to the providers above without logging, caching, or storing photo data. Vercel operates under GDPR-compliant data processing terms. See vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription status. Receives anonymized purchase data from Apple. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google): Provides anonymized usage analytics and crash reporting. No photos or personal identifiers are sent to Firebase. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Records anonymous app interactions (taps, scrolls, screens) with photos masked, to help us improve usability. Not used in the EEA, UK or Switzerland. See privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.
  • Apple (StoreKit): Handles all payment processing for subscriptions and in-app purchases. We never receive your payment details.

4. Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data on our own servers. Our AI provider OpenAI keeps photos and generated images for up to 30 days (to deliver your result and for abuse monitoring) and then deletes them; it does not use them to train AI models. All user preferences, saved looks, and app data are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all local data.

5. Data Sharing and Consent

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Data is only shared with the third-party services listed above, solely for the purposes described.

Before your selfie is sent to our AI provider for processing, the app shows an explicit in-app consent dialog that names the third-party processor and summarizes this policy. You must tap "I Agree" to proceed; you can tap "Privacy Policy" to read this policy in full, or "Cancel" to decline. No photo data leaves your device until you explicitly consent.

6. Children's Privacy

SwapHair AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not store personal data on servers, there is generally no personal data to access, modify, or delete on our end. You can:

  • Delete all local app data by uninstalling the app
  • Clear cached images from within the app settings
  • Manage or cancel your subscription through Apple Settings

8. Security

All data transmission between the app, our relay server and our processing providers uses HTTPS encryption. Our relay server processes photos in memory and never writes them to persistent storage; our AI provider retains them only as described in Section 4.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
